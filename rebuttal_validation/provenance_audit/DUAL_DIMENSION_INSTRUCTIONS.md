# Dual-Dimension Provenance Audit

Do not edit the retrieved claim text, including factual errors. Label those errors `F`.

## CLAIM label: public knowability anywhere
- A: exact claim was publicly knowable before cutoff.
- B: exact claim became knowable only after cutoff.
- C: insufficient evidence.
- F: claim is factually false/unsupported as stated.

## SOURCE label: cited-page version compliance
- A: exact information appears in the cited source's pre-cutoff version.
- B: exact information appears only in a post-cutoff version.
- C: source history is insufficient.

The professor's prior labels are prefilled under `professor_claim_label`. The professor should fill only `professor_source_label`. The student should review both of their prefilled columns. Then jointly fill the adjudicated columns.
