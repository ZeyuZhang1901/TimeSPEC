Japan’s Abe Aims for Summit With South Korean President

Abe, Park haven’t held a formal two-way meeting since they took power in their respective countries

By
Yuka Hayashi in Tokyo and
Alastair Gale in Seoul
  • Alastair Gale
    The Wall Street Journal
    CANCEL
  • BiographyAlastair Gale
  • @AlastairGale
  • alastair.gale@wsj.com
June 22, 2015 8:01 a.m. ET

Japan and South Korea pledged to resume top-level dialogue to repair their frazzled ties, taking advantage of celebrations Monday to mark the 50th anniversary of their diplomatic relations.

Japanese Prime Minister Shinzo Abe said he hoped to have a formal summit meeting with South Korean President Park Geun-hye soon, a move that would represent a big step forward for the two nations that have fought bitterly over wartime history...
